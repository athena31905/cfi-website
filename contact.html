Funding & Opportunities

Support built around serious research.

As part of the Graduate School of Convergence Security, we provide financial and professional support so that students can focus on ambitious questions, meaningful collaboration, and sustained research.

01

Tuition Support

Full tuition coverage is provided to incoming students for their first semester. For the remaining semesters, performance-based scholarships equivalent to tuition may be awarded.

02

Research Funding

Additional research assistant compensation may be provided according to each student’s level of participation in lab-specific research projects.

03

Professional Opportunities

Students may receive opportunities to participate in internships, attend major academic conferences, and join overseas training programs.
